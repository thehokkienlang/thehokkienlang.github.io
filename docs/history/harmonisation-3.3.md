# Orthography v3.3 Hanri Assignments

Author-confirmed on 2026-11-08 against Tangliengim Orthography v3.3, section 4.5.

| Previous form | Hanri | Dictionary reading | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 감ˋ (question marker) | 噉 | 감2 | 81 ruby uses in songs and stories, one song title |
| 今 read 달 | 𦤾 | 달1 | 8 ruby uses; 到𦤾 and 𦤾仔; new standalone 𦤾 |

Mixed running text and display titles use the new Hanri. Authored
pronunciations, tone marks, Lomari, links and routes are unchanged, so
`/songs/請問릐감欲做꽈에ᄁힻ흐뗀/` keeps its address. 今 keeps its 긴 and 김
readings, including 今仔日, and lexical 敢 and 感 are separate entries.

Excluded: the pure-Hangul Lychee Mirror text, apostrophised neutral domains,
first song headings used by the title-reading parser, and tables or
reference material, as in the v3.2.1 scope.

`harmonisation-3.3-audit.json` records reversible page edits and
`harmonisation-3.3-dictionary.json` records the dictionary transaction and
retired IDs. 𦤾 is served from the Plangothic P1 V2.9.5795 content subset and
噉 from the Noto Sans TC v3.2.1 subset.
