Existing low-tone 當 is reused. No citation reading is inferred from lyric
sandhi. The new 丈夫 entry means man/male in this reading, not a guessed
Mandarin husband gloss.

Mixed running text and display titles use Hanri; authored pronunciations,
tone marks, Lomari, links and routes remain unchanged. Existing ruby,
apostrophised domains, foreign transcriptions and teaching material are
excluded, as in the original harmonisation scope. Historical source headings
remain available to the title-reading parser.

`harmonisation-3.2.1-audit.json` records reversible edits against the current
authored pages. It is separate from the older v3.2 ledger, whose hashes can
predate subsequent page edits. `harmonisation-3.2.1-dictionary.json` records
the validated dictionary transaction and exact ID retirements. Superseded
active Hangul records are removed, archived as defunct, and their IDs remain
reserved. References and category/priority memberships follow replacements.
