Song sound and mood picker

Pick a sound and one or two moods for each song. Yellow boxes are guesses or empty and need your choice; they turn white once you change or confirm them. Choices are saved in this browser each time you change one, and when you press Save; the time of the last save shows beside the button. Download choices also makes a file you can bring back with Load from file. When you are done, press Download choices and save the file into the docs folder, or press Copy choices and paste them into the chat. Years are for reference only: "~" marks an estimate, and none have been verified.
