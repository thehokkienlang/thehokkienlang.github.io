Song links and page titles

Enter each song's standardised mixed Hanri–Hangul title without apostrophes or tone marks, for example 生份에릐. The /songs/ prefix and final slash are supplied automatically. Leave undecided titles blank.

The page title can include spaces, punctuation and tone marks. Titles made entirely of Hanri are copied from the chosen URL; fill in the remaining titles yourself. Your work is saved in this browser where storage is available. Use Download completed form to return your choices.
